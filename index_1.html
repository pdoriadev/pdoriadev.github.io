Games are art. Games are business. I make art to make money to make art. I do not like forever games. I do not like poorly designed incremental games that pull players along a treadmill of hollow extrinsic rewards. I like puzzle games, well-written RPGs, some shooter puzzles, co-operative games. It better have a strong narrative premise, some level of puzzle, and / or be fun with friends. Actually fun with friends. I like games that are about something. Games that feel like they were made by a team, not by a machine.
